Program

A one-day workshop built around interaction, with invited keynotes, contributed papers, posters, the evaluation challenge, and a closing panel. February 22 or 23, 2027, at the Palais des Congrès de Montréal.

  1. Home
  2. Program

Format

Four invited keynotes of 30 minutes each, spanning the state of AI Scientist systems, benchmarking, verification, and simulation. Two contributed paper sessions. Two poster sessions held during the coffee breaks. A session presenting results of the AI Scientist Evaluation Challenge. A closing panel with open discussion.

Sessions

Keynotes

Four invited talks spanning the state of AI Scientist systems, benchmarking, verification, and simulation. Speakers will be announced on the keynotes page.

Paper sessions

Two contributed sessions, the first on benchmarking and system evaluation, the second on verification, simulation, and human-AI collaboration. Accepted papers appear on the accepted papers page.

Poster sessions

Two poster sessions run during the coffee breaks, giving every accepted paper a slot for extended discussion.

Challenge session

Results of the AI Scientist Evaluation Challenge, with spotlight talks from participating teams in both tracks.

Closing panel

An open discussion under the question that runs through the whole day, whether AI can truly judge AI.
