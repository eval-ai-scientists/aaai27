Getting to the workshop

The workshop is part of the 41st AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, held in Montréal from February 16 to February 23, 2027. Registration, accommodation, and travel information are on the AAAI-27 conference site. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present in person, per AAAI policy.
