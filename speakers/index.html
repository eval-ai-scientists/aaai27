Keynotes

Four invited talks of 30 minutes each, spanning the state of AI Scientist systems, benchmarking, verification, and simulation.

Speakers to be announced

Invitations are in progress. The four keynote slots and their topics are set out below, and this page is updated as speakers confirm.

K1

The State of AI Scientists

09:15–09:45

Where autonomous research systems stand today, what they can complete without human involvement, and where they break.

K2

Benchmarking Autonomous Research Agents

09:45–10:15

End-to-end and stage-wise measurement, what current benchmarks capture, and what they miss.

K3

Verifying AI-Generated Science

15:30–16:00

Reproducibility, fact-checking, and statistical validity of findings produced by autonomous systems.

K4

Simulation as a Testbed for Autonomous Discovery

16:00–16:30

Controlled environments in which AI Scientists can be watched, stress-tested, and compared before their outputs reach a reviewer.
