Organizers

Four organizers from four institutions across North America and Asia, with perspectives that deliberately do not overlap. Each maps onto one of the workshop themes.

  1. Home
  2. Organizers

Tianyu Shi

University of Toronto

Primary contact

Tianyu Shi works on LLM agents, multi-agent systems, and world models. The CARE work, on controlling LLM-generated policies through auditable review of evidence in scientific experimentation, speaks directly to auditable evaluation of AI-generated science, and the DynaWeb work on model-based reinforcement learning of web agents adds depth in evaluating agentic systems.

tianyu.shi@utoronto.ca

Bang Liu

Université de Montréal and Mila, CIFAR AI Chair

Organizer

Bang Liu works on LLM agents, natural language processing, and AI for Science. The AFlow work on automating agentic workflow generation (ICLR 2025) and Data Interpreter (ACL 2025) probe the capabilities and limits of agentic workflows in science, and the Honeycomb system (EMNLP 2024) reflects hands-on deployment of LLM agents in materials science.

bang.liu@umontreal.ca

Fan Zhou

Shanghai University of Finance and Economics

Organizer

Fan Zhou works on reinforcement learning and causal inference. NeurIPS work on off-policy evaluation in causal reinforcement learning and on optimal treatment allocation for efficient policy evaluation supplies the statistical and causal foundations needed to assess AI-generated findings beyond surface metrics.

zhou.fan@mail.shufe.edu.cn

Steve Drew

University of Calgary

Organizer

Steve Drew is an Associate Professor of Electrical and Software Engineering at the Schulich School of Engineering, University of Calgary, and leads the Distributed Learning and Orchestration (DENOS) Lab. The group builds agentic simulations of complex real-world settings, including EDSim, an emergency department simulation in which autonomous patient, nurse, and physician agents surface operational bottlenecks, and AgentEvac, which models wildfire evacuations with psychologically grounded LLM agents. This work anchors the workshop's simulation and testbed theme.

steve.drew@ucalgary.ca
